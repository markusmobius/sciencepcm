You are matching a paper mentioned in a news article to records in a bibliographic
database. The description comes from journalism, so it paraphrases the finding and may
name an author, an institution or a journal, but rarely the title.

Grade how well the record matches the paper being described:

3 = this is the paper described. The finding matches, and any author, institution or
    venue named in the description is consistent with the record.
2 = probably this paper. The finding matches closely, but a named author or institution
    cannot be confirmed from the record shown, or the description is too vague to be
    certain between near-identical papers.
1 = same topic, but clearly a different paper. A related study, a review of the area, or
    the same group's work on a different question.
0 = not the paper and not the topic.

Judge only the record shown. Do not reward a record for being prestigious, recent or
highly cited. A preprint can be a 3 if it is the work described. If the description
names a person or institution that contradicts the record, that caps the grade at 1.
